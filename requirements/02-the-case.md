# The case: *The Adventure of the Speckled Band*

Arthur Conan Doyle, first published in *The Strand Magazine*, February 1892.
Set in April 1883. Public domain ([sources.md](sources.md)).

## Summary (spoilers — this is the solution design)

Helen Stoner arrives at Baker Street at dawn, terrified. Two years ago her
twin sister Julia died in her locked bedroom at Stoke Moran, weeks before her
wedding. Her last words were "the speckled band". Now Helen is engaged, and
building work has forced her to sleep in Julia's old room. Last night she
heard the same low whistle Julia heard before she died.

Their stepfather, Dr Grimesby Roylott, controls their late mother's money,
and each daughter is entitled to a share when she marries. Holmes finds that
Julia's bell-pull is a dummy hanging from a ventilator into Roylott's room,
and that the bed is clamped to the floor beneath it. Roylott had trained a
venomous swamp adder from India to climb through the ventilator, down the
cord onto the bed, and return to his whistle. During a night vigil Holmes
drives the snake back through the ventilator; it bites Roylott, who dies.

## The will (a key motive puzzle)

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Mother's income at her death | close to £1,100 a year |
| Income now (falling agricultural prices) | not more than £750 a year |
| Each daughter's share on marriage | £250 a year |
| If both married | Roylott keeps very little |

Helen remembers the fortune as "not less than £1,000 a year"; the will shows
the exact figures. The player computes the effect of each marriage
([04-deduction-chain.md](04-deduction-chain.md), D-04).
