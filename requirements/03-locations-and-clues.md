## Layout of the bedroom wing (ground floor)

```text
 main block
     │ side door
 ┌───┴──────────────────── corridor (narrow windows) ───────────────┐
 │  [L-05 Roylott] ══ventilator══ [L-06 Julia] │ [L-07 Helen]       │
 └──────── windows with barred shutters onto the lawn ───────────────┘
```

No doors connect the bedrooms. Each opens onto the corridor; each window
opens onto the lawn and is shuttered and barred at night. The chimney is
barred with iron staples. Walls and floors are solid.
